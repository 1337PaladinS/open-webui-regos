Sec. 24-15.4. - Technical Reports; Professional Engineer or Professional Geologist required. 

All applicable portions of the technical plans, reports, proposals or studies required pursuant to subsection 24-44(2) shall be signed and sealed by a professional engineer licensed in the State of Florida or professional geologist licensed in the State of Florida. 

(Ord. No. 04-214, §§ 1, 5, 12-2-04; Ord. No. 24-92, § 1, 9-4-24)
