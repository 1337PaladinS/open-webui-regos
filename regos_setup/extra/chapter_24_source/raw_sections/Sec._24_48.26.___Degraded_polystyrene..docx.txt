Sec. 24-48.26. - Degraded polystyrene. 

For all facilities which are subject to a County marine facilities operating permit pursuant to this chapter, such permit shall require the removal, replacement or repair of any unencapsulated polystyrene where such polystyrene shows evidence of degradation, disintegration, shredding, or other damage, as determined in the discretion of the Director. 

(Ord. No. 18-10, § 4, 2-6-18)
