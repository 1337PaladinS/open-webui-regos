Sec. 24-42.1. - Tertiary treatment requirements. 

All new sewage treatment plants and industrial liquid waste treatment facilities, except those discharging to approved ocean outfalls or deep disposal wells, shall provide for nutrient removal and the following: 

(1)  Ninety-five (95) percent removal of the influent biochemical oxygen demand (BOD) concentration which will result in an effluent concentration which shall not exceed 15.0 mg/l. 

(2)  Ninety-five (95) percent removal of the influent total suspended solids (TSS) concentration which will result in an effluent concentration which shall not exceed 15.0 mg/l. 

(3)  Effluent discharged to surface waters shall not exceed 3.0 mg/l of methylene blue active substance (MBAS). 

(4)  Effluent discharged to surface waters shall not exceed 1.0 mg/l of phosphorous (P). 

(5)  All other applicable standards in Section 24-42(2) shall be met. 

(Ord. No. 04-214, §§ 1, 5, 12-2-04; Ord. No. 08-55, § 2, 5-6-08)
