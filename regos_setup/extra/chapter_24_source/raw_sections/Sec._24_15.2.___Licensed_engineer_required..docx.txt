Sec. 24-15.2. - Licensed engineer required. 

(1)  The drawings, specifications, and other data submitted with an application required by this chapter shall be prepared by one or more professional engineers licensed in the State of Florida. 

(2)  All materials required to be submitted by a Florida licensed engineer shall be signed and sealed pursuant to chapter 61G15-23 of the Florida Administrative Code or other applicable state regulation. 

(3)  Any application that does not comply with this section shall be deemed incomplete and subject to denial as such. 

(Ord. No. 04-214, §§ 1, 5, 12-2-04; Ord. No. 08-55, § 2, 5-6-08; Ord. No. 24-92, § 1, 9-4-24) 

Editor's note— Ord. No. 24-92, § 1, adopted September 4, 2024, amended the title of § 24-15.2 by changing "registered" to "licensed."
