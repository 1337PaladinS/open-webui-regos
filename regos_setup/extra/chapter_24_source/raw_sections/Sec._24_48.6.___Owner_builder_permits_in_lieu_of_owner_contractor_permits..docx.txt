Sec. 24-48.6. - Owner-builder permits in lieu of owner-contractor permits. 

At the discretion of the Director or the Director's designee, owner-builder permits may be issued for the following types of work requiring a class I permit, and thereby waive the requirement that the permit be issued jointly to the owner and a contractor holding an applicable certificate of competency: 

(1)  Repair of seawalls: Repair of seawalls or bulkheads at the mean high water line or at their existing location (excluding pile-driving operations and/or panel installations). 

(2)  Placement of riprap: The placement of riprap in front of an existing or new seawall, bulkhead or shoreline, provided there is no adverse environmental impact associated with the project. 

(3)  Where the upland property is zoned as single-family residential: 

(a)  Repair, replacement or restoration of docks which are limited to or less than their original dimensions and which together with associated tie-up facilities, do not protrude into the water more than twenty-five (25) percent of the width of the waterway (excluding pile-driving operations and any associated dredging and filling). 

(b)  Installation of buoys, when it is determined that the proposed installation will not present a hazard to navigation. 

(4)  Other work that is nonstructural in nature. 

(Ord. No. 04-214, §§ 1, 5, 12-2-04; Ord. No. 08-55, § 2, 5-6-08; Ord. No. 12-58, § 5, 7-3-12)
