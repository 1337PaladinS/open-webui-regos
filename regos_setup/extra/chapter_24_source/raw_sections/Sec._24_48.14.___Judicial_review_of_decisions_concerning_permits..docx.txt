Sec. 24-48.14. - Judicial review of decisions concerning permits. 

Any person aggrieved by any decision of the Board of County Commissioners with regard to the granting, denial, granting with limitations, restrictions or conditions, or the revocation or modification of any permit in this article may seek judicial review in accordance with the Florida Rules of Appellate Procedure. 

(Ord. No. 04-214, §§ 1, 5, 12-2-04)
