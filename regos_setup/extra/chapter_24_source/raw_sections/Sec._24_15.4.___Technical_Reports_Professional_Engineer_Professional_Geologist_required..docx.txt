Sec. 24-15.4. - Technical Reports/Professional Engineer/Professional Geologist required. 

All applicable portions of the technical plans, reports, proposals or studies required as set forth in Section 24-44(2) shall be signed and sealed by a licensed professional engineer registered in the State of Florida or licensed professional geologist registered in the State of Florida. 

(Ord. No. 04-214, §§ 1, 5, 12-2-04)
