Sec. 24-41.15. - Adoption by reference of Federal exceptions. 

The exceptions set forth in Section 604(d), (e), (f), and (g) and in Section 605(d) of the Federal Clean Air Act Amendments of 1990 are adopted by reference as if said exceptions were fully set forth in this chapter. 

(Ord. No. 04-214, §§ 1, 5, 12-2-04; Ord. No. 08-55, § 2, 5-6-08)
