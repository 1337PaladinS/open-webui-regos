Sec. 24-41.16. - Spraying of substances containing asbestos. 

It shall be unlawful within Miami-Dade County, for any person, firm or corporation, to cause or to permit the spraying of any substance containing asbestos, as defined in Section 24-5, in or upon any building, structure, column, frame, floor, ceiling or other portion, part or member thereof, during its construction, reconstruction, alteration or repair; provided, however, that such enclosed factories, buildings or structures in which the fabrication or manufacture of products containing asbestos is carried on shall not be subject to this provision. 

(Ord. No. 04-214, §§ 1, 5, 12-2-04)
