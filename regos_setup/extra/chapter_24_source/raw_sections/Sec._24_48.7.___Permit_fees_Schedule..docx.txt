Sec. 24-48.7. - Permit fees—Schedule. 

The Department shall charge and collect application and permit fees at the rate established by separate administrative order which shall not become effective until approved by the Board of County Commissioners. 

(Ord. No. 04-214, §§ 1, 5, 12-2-04; Ord. No. 08-55, § 2, 5-6-08)
