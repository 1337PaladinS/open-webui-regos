Sec. 24-48.22. - Biscayne Bay and environs designated aquatic park and conservation area. 

(1)  In recognition that is in the interest of the public welfare to protect and preserve unique, natural, aesthetic and recreational values, Biscayne Bay and its environs is hereby declared to be an "aquatic park and conservation area" for the use and benefit of the citizens of Miami-Dade County. 

(2)  The County Manager is hereby empowered to develop a plan for the protection and preservation of said "aquatic park and conservation area" including the initiation and coordination of appropriate research and analysis, the development of both short and long-range plans, and the promulgation of rules and regulations which, after ratification by the Board of County Commissioners and the appropriate agencies of the State of Florida and of the federal government, shall have the force and effect of law. 

(Ord. No. 04-214, §§ 1, 5, 12-2-04; Ord. No. 08-55, § 2, 5-6-08)
