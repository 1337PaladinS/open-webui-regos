Sec. 24-48.20. - North Trail Basin Plan. 

Except as provided in Section 24-48.3(5)(e), all work performed in the North Trail Basin shall be consistent with the North Trail Basin Plan, which is hereby adopted by reference in its entirety. The Clerk of the Board of County Commissioners is directed to keep a copy of the North Trail Basin Plan on file as an attachment to this ordinance. All work in the North Trail Basin shall be consistent with the goals, guidelines, standards, and project design criteria set forth in the North Trail Basin Plan to ensure the maintenance of, or mitigation for the loss of, biological resources. All work in the North Trail Basin shall conform to the North Trail Basin cut and fill criteria which have been set forth in Department Technical Document 89-4 to ensure proper water management. 

(Ord. No. 04-214, §§ 1, 5, 12-2-04; Ord. No. 08-55, § 2, 5-6-08)
