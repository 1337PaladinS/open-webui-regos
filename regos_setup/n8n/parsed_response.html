Summary

The specific BOD (Biochemical Oxygen Demand) limits for wastewater discharge in Miami-Dade County Chapter 24 are not explicitly stated in the retrieved sections. However, the regulatory framework requires compliance with effluent standards under Section 24-42(2) (referenced in [G2]) and pretreatment standards under Section 24-42.4 ([G1]). Compliance testing for BOD removal is mandated, and sampling must follow methods outlined in Section 24-44.2 ([G2]).

Regulatory Analysis

1. BOD Compliance Context

  • While the exact BOD numerical limit is not directly quoted in the retrieved sections, Section 24-44.2(1) ([G2]) states that samples for compliance with effluent standards (including BOD) must be taken at the point past which no further treatment occurs.
  • Section 24-44.2(5) ([G2]) explicitly mentions "percent removal of BOD and suspended solids," indicating that BOD limits are tied to removal efficiency requirements.

2. Pretreatment Standards

  • Section 24-42.4 ([G1]) governs pretreatment standards for industrial users discharging into publicly owned treatment works (POTWs). It prohibits discharges that cause "interference" or "pass-through" of pollutants, which may indirectly regulate BOD levels.

3. Stormwater and FOG Provisions

  • Sections 24-42.8 ([G3]) and 24-42.6 ([G4]) address stormwater and Fats, Oils, and Grease (FOG) controls but do not specify BOD limits.
